## [IPRODAM3D](https://sites.google.com/site/iprodamgroup): Image Processing and Data Mining 3D research group

![](../images/iprodam3d.png)

[IPRODAM3D](https://sites.google.com/site/iprodamgroup) research group figures out cutting edge problems related to the areas of image processing, geometry processing, and artificial intelligence. Since 2013, professors and students from La Salle University and the National University of Saint Augustine in the city of Arequipa meet to delve into the delights of science under the leadership of [Dr. Cristian López Del Alamo](https://sites.google.com/site/cristianlopezdelalamo).
