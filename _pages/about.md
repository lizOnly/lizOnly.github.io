I earned my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Zurich, advised by Prof. Renato Pajarola, and was an early-stage researcher in the [EVOCATION](https://evocation.eu/) innovative training network. 
Previously, I completed my MSc in Computer Science from Federal Fluminense University (Niterói, Brazil) and a BSc from the National University of Saint Augustine (Arequipa, Peru).

My research bridges computer graphics, 3D computer vision, and machine learning. I develop geometry-based and neural methods for 3D reconstruction, combining geometric precision with the flexibility of learning-based approaches. 

Outside of work, I enjoy bouldering, painting, and writing.
